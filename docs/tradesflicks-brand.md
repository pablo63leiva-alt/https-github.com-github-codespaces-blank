## 1. Brand

- **Handle:** @tradesflicks (TikTok/IG/YT consistently)
- **Voice:** older sibling who went into the trades, not a career counselor. Rides the line between hype and honest. Short, visual, stat-first.
- **Format:** 9:16 vertical, 15–45s, captions baked in (most watch muted).
- **Signature:** every video ends with the same 3-word CTA → "Take the quiz" (link in bio → quiz.html).
- **Hook style:** first 1–2s is a question or a counter-statement ("College called. It's the wrong memo.")
