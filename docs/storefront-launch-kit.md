## 5. Pricing Rationale & PABLO Notes

### 5a. Why $12 (list price) in the 16–24 impulse range
- **Impulse-buy band.** $9–$19 is the proven "cheap enough to not think twice, valuable
  enough to feel real" range for PDF buyers 16–24. $12 sits at the psychological midpoint:
  under the $15 "nice dinner" threshold, above the $5 "throwaway" zone.
- **Price anchors to value.** The guide promises a career path worth $60K–$100K+/yr. A $12
  price anchors ~0.01–0.02% of one year's journey-level income — trivially justified.
- **vs. alternatives:** it undercuts both trade school brochures and career-counselor paid
  services (many run $200+), while outperforming free blog content (paid = you take it
  seriously + we can afford email nurture).
- **Revenue math to target:**
  - Launch-phase $9 × 200 sales = **$1,800** (kickoff capital).
  - Steady-state $12 × 100/mo = **$1,200/mo**. By Dec 22, 2026 (~15 weeks out) the guide +
  - bundle can realistically clear **~$9,000–$15,000** of the $50K target on its own.
  - The remaining ~$35K comes from the ROI calculator, a cohort/premium upsell, and affiliate
    scale — this kit only ships product #1.
- **Discount ceiling:** never drop below $9 permanently — below that, it reads as low-value
  and undermines the follow-up products' pricing.

### 5b. PABLO action required
- **Gumroad account creation + payout details** (bank + tax). This is the single blocking
  dependency for going live. Everything else in this kit is copy-paste ready.
- Suggested opening: trade the account's "creator" seat to PABLO, and give Stark/Vision
  editor access (Gumroad supports team invites on the free plan's product level is limited —
