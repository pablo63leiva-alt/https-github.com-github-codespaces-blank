### Engagement Rules
- Reply to every comment within 60 minutes of posting (boosts algorithm reach)
- First-hour replies are critical — stay online after posting
- Pin the most-engaged comment to drive conversation

### Cross-Post Workflow
1. Film one vertical video → TikTok first (primary)
2. Re-cut → IG Reel (same format, adjust captions)
3. Re-cut → YouTube Short (add 5–10s of extra hook)
4. Screenshot stat from video → IG carousel / X graphic
5. Link in bio → quiz.html with UTM tracking
