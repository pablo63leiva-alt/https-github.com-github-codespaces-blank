## 3. Link-Building Strategies

### 3.1 Create Linkable Assets

These are high-value pages designed to attract links naturally:

| Asset | Description | Target Links From |
|-------|-------------|-------------------|
| **"State of Trade Careers 2026" Report** | Original data compilation — salaries by state, job growth projections, apprenticeship completion rates, debt comparisons | Career sites, education blogs, local news, government |
| **Infographic: "Trade School vs College: The Real Cost"** | Side-by-side 4-year comparison: total cost, debt, earning timeline, job placement rate | Education blogs, personal finance sites, social sharing |
| **Interactive Salary Calculator** | Users input trade + location → get salary range, cost of living adjustment, projected 5-year earnings | Link roundups, career sites, school resource pages |
| **Trade School Directory** | Searchable list of accredited trade programs by state and trade type | Education directories, guidance counselor pages |
| **"Is the Trades Right for Me?" Quiz** | 10-question assessment that matches users to a trade career | Social media, student communities, career sites |

### 3.2 HARO (Help a Reporter Out)

**Setup:**
- Register at helpareporter.com (free tier)
- Set alerts for: "skilled trades," "trade school," "vocational education," "apprenticeship," "blue collar careers," "student debt alternatives"
- Respond 2-3x per week

**Response strategy:**
- Lead with data: "Our research across 12 trades shows..."
- Be quotable: Give reporters a clean soundbite they can paste
- Include credentials: "TradeLift, a free career resource for thousands of young adults"
- Target 50-word responses for speed, expand if they follow up

**Target publications:**
- Local newspapers (trade stories are regional gold)
- Forbes, Business Insider, MarketWatch (workforce stories)
- Education publications (EdSurge, Inside Higher Ed)
- Trade publications (Constructor Magazine, Electrical Contractor)

### 3.3 Expert Roundups

**Strategy:** Create a "15 Career Counselors Share Their Advice for Trade-School Bound Students" post on TradeLift's blog.

**Process:**
1. Identify 20-30 career counselors, trade instructors, and working tradespeople
2. Email a single question: "What's the #1 thing you'd tell a teenager considering trade school?"
3. Compile responses into a blog post
4. Every participant shares and links back
5. Net result: 15-20 backlinks + social amplification

### 3.4 Scholarship Link Building (If Budget Allows)

**If a small budget ($500-$1,000) is available:**
- Create the "TradeLift Skilled Trades Scholarship" — $500/year
- Write a 500-word essay prompt: "Why I'm Pursuing a Career in the Trades"
- Submit to 200+ scholarship directories (all free to list)
- Each listing = a .edu or high-DA backlink
- Cost: ~$500/year for the scholarship + free time to manage applications

**If no budget:** Skip this strategy. Focus on free methods.

## 4. Content for Link Building

### 4.1 "State of Trade Careers 2026" Report

**Status:** To be created  
**Target publication date:** October 2026  
**Word count:** 2,000-3,000 words + data visualizations  

**Outline:**
1. Executive Summary — 3 key takeaways
2. Methodology — Sources: BLS OES, OSHA data, NCES, TradeLift quiz data (thousands of career-quiz responses)
3. Salary Trends by Trade — Table + chart for all 12 trades
4. Geographic Hotspots — Top 10 states for each trade
5. The Debt Comparison — Trade school vs 4-year degree: total cost, time to ROI
6. Job Outlook 2026-2036 — BLS projections with TradeLift commentary
7. The Skills Gap — 750K open positions analysis
8. Demographic Shifts — Who's entering trades (age, gender, background)
9. Recommendations — For students, educators, policymakers
10. Full Data Tables — Downloadable CSV

**Distribution:**
- Publish on TradeLift blog with dedicated landing page
- Pitch to BLS.gov, OSHA.gov, state workforce agencies as supplementary data
- Offer exclusives to Indeed/Glassdoor for early access
- Share with all Tier 3 blog targets as a data source

### 4.2 Infographic: "Trade School vs College: The Real Cost"

**Dimensions:** 800x2000px (standard infographic)  
**Tool:** Canva (free) or Figma (free)  

**Data Points to Visualize:**
- Total 4-year cost: Trade school ($10K-$30K) vs College ($100K-$200K+)
- Average student debt: Trade ($0-$8K) vs College ($37K avg)
- Time to first paycheck: Trade (immediate via apprenticeship) vs College (4+ years)
- Job placement rate comparison
- 10-year earnings trajectory (trade wins by year 3-4 typically)
- Job satisfaction scores by sector

**Distribution:**
- Embed on TradeLift blog
- Submit to Infographic Journal, Visual.ly, Infogram
- Pitch to personal finance blogs as a free resource
- Share on Pinterest, Reddit infographics communities

### 4.3 Interactive Tool: "Salary Calculator by Trade"

**Tech:** Static HTML/JS (surge.sh compatible)  
**Features:**
- Dropdown: Select trade (12 options)
- Dropdown: Select state
- Output: Starting salary, mid-career salary, top 10% salary, cost of living adjustment
- Comparison mode: "How does this compare to a [degree] in [field]?"
- Share button → generates unique URL with results

**Link-building value:**
- Embeddable widget other sites can add (with backlink)
- Tool roundup submissions (AlternativeTo, SaaSHub)
- Career site referrals

### 4.4 Scholarship Page

**Only if budget allows ($500-$1,000/year)**
- Landing page: /scholarship
- Application form: Google Forms
- Requirements: Essay on trade career motivation
- Deadline: Annual (e.g., June 1)
- Announcement: Blog post with winner story
- Directory submissions: 200+ scholarship sites
