## 6. Revenue Projection Model — 3 Scenarios

Shared mechanics: list capture ≈ **2% of visitors** (newsletter + quiz + exit-modal blend); Amazon ≈ **2% click→order** at **~$4 avg commission** (~$80 basket × ~5%×blended, matches early-pillar math); product CVR ≈ **2–4% of list** over a launch window; merch = low-margin add-on.

| Metric | Conservative | Base | Stretch |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cumulative visitors (Sep→Dec) | ~15K | ~42K | ~135K |
| Email subscribers by Dec 22 | ~300 | ~830 | ~3,500 |
| Affiliate clicks→commissions | ~550 → ~$110 | ~3.5K → ~$690 | ~20K → ~$4.7K |
| Digital products | ~$280 | ~$3,000 | ~$41,500 |
| Merch (Etsy/Printful) | ~$100 | ~$600 | ~$2,300 |
| Sponsorship / school bulk | $0 | $0 | ~$1,500 |
| **Cumulative revenue** | **~$0.5K** | **~$4.3K** | **~$50K** |

**Stretch trigger (required — the math is honest):** one breakout event inside Nov–Dec — one viral short (≥2M views) or 3+ bulk school-pack deals — feeding a **~25–40K-visitor week with a paid product live to convert**. Without a breakout, $50K in 100 days from zero traffic is not achievable via organic compounding; the plan's job is to be **breakout-ready** (products, funnel, and list built before the spike hits).

**Base already assumes aggressive SEO + list growth and DOES NOT reach the $50K headline — that is intentional** (see REPORT to Pablo).

**The single most important number to watch each week: net-new email subscribers.** It is the run-rate that compounds into every pillar (repeat affiliate traffic, launch buyers, merch audience). Where it lives today: Formspree submission counts (forms `xqpkvyjg` + `xzebljww`). **Rule: two consecutive weeks under target → trigger a content/social boost and re-check traffic source quality.**
