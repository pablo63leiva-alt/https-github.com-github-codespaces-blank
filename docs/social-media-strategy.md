# TradeLift Social Media Strategy — 90-Day Content Calendar

**Owner:** Banner (social media specialist)
**Mission:** Drive traffic to https://tradelift.surge.sh, build the email list via the quiz funnel (`quiz.html`), and create shareable short-form content for teens/young adults (16-24) considering skilled-trade careers.
**Funnel:** Social post → link in bio / QR / comment → `quiz.html` → email capture (`conversion-funnel.md`) → roadmap nurture sequence → revenue.
**Live base URL:** `https://tradelift.surge.sh` (append UTM parameters per section 7)

---

## 1. Platform Strategy

### TikTok — primary channel (highest reach with 16-24s)

| Format | Use case |
|--------|----------|
| Day-in-the-life (15-30s) | Trade worker POV clips + text overlay ("POV: you apprentice as an electrician and it beats your table job") |
| Salary reveals | "How much do electricians REALLY make?" — flash salary bands, cite real state data |
| Myth-busting | Setup a college myth (e.g., "You need a degree to make $100K") and flip it on screen |
| Stitches/duets | Stitch other trade creators answering "why did you choose the trades?" |
| Quiz hooks | "I took a 2-min trade quiz and got [Trade] — what are YOU?" → link to quiz.html |

**Posting cadence:** 5-7/week. Native captions, on-screen text (many watch muted), 2-3 hashtags max.

### Instagram — conversion + community hub

| Format | Use case |
|--------|----------|
| Feed carousels | Trade vs trade salary/requirements comparisons ("Electrician vs Welder: which pays more?") — slide 6 = "Take the quiz" |
| Reels | Re-pack top TikTok content, or fast "3 tools every apprentice needs" tips |
| Stories | Polls ("Would you date a welder?" / "College or apprenticeship?"), quizzes, countdown to new content, link sticker → quiz.html |
| Saved/Shareable | Salary stat graphics people repost ("Electricians: $60K-$80K, no degree, no debt") |
| Highlights | Permanent "Money", "Trades", "Quiz" highlight bins for new followers |

### Twitter / X — authority + community engagement

| Format | Use case |
|--------|----------|
| Threads | "How to become an electrician in 12 weeks (no degree)" 6-10 tweet threads with site/blog links |
| Polls | "Which trade would you try?" — polls feed both engagement and future content ideas |
| Community engagement | Reply to trade-school/building-trades hashtags, quote-tweet labor-market news |
| Salary data drops | Share BLS data graphics with a hot take ("Highest-paying jobs without a degree > most majors") |
| Engagement | RT creator content, comment on #SkilledTrades, respond to every reply within the hour |

### YouTube Shorts — search discoverability + longer how-tos

| Format | Use case |
|--------|----------|
| Shorts | 45-60s: "Day in the life of a plumber apprentice", trade intro explainers (links back to trades.html/blog) |
| Long-form (optional, month 3) | One 8-12 min video/quarter: "Trade School vs College — the real math" (transcript doubles as SEO blog content) |
| Playlists | Group Shorts by trade + by pillar for channel organization |

**Cross-posting rule:** One asset, four cuts. Film vertical once, then re-cut into TikTok (muted-first), Reel (same format), Short (extra 15s of hook), and screenshot/thread material for X. Maximum output, minimum filming.

## 3. 90-Day Content Calendar

### Phase map

| Phase | Weeks | Theme | Goal | Primary pillar focus |
|-------|-------|-------|------|----------------------|
| Foundation | 1-4 | Brand awareness, introduce TradeLift | 0 → 1,000 combined followers; define voice | P2 (day-in-the-life), P4 (myth-busting) |
| Education | 5-8 | Trade spotlights + salary data | 1,000 → 3,000 followers; site traffic up | P1 (salary), P3 (vs college), P5 (tools) |
| Conversion | 9-12 | CTA-heavy, quiz promotion, email capture | 3,000+ followers; email list growth; link clicks | P6 (success stories), quiz formats |

| Day | TikTok | Instagram | X | YouTube Shorts |
|-----|--------|-----------|------|----------------|

### Phase 1 — Foundation (Weeks 1-4)

Goals: establish voice, build first audience, prove the niche. Post consistently (even if thin), reply fast, bank on myth-busting + salary hooks which travel.

| Week | Focus | Sample posts | CTA |
|------|-------|--------------|-----|
| 1 | Introducing TradeLift + who the trades are for | "Why we made TradeLift" (founder POV); "12 trades you should know about"; poll "which trade are you curious about?" | Quiz teaser |
| 2 | Myth-busting blast | "You need a degree for a good paycheck" FALSE; "Trades are dead-end" FALSE; DITL: carpenter | Quiz + link in bio |
| 3 | Salary curiosity | First "REALLY make?" salary video; "highest-paying jobs without a degree" carousel | Blog post link (`blog/highest-paying-jobs-without-a-degree.html`) |
| 4 | Test + double down | Quiz hook post ("which trade are you?"); analyze best formats; re-shoot variants | Quiz + share-result cards |

Deliverables by end of Phase 1: 4+ reels/shorts/week, 1 X thread, 1 carousel/week, voice guide documented, week 4 winners flagged.

### Phase 2 — Education (Weeks 5-8)

Goals: become the trade-info destination. Trade spotlights, real state-level salary data, school-vs-college math. Every post links deeper into the site (blog, trades.html).

| Week | Focus | Sample posts | CTA |
|------|-------|--------------|-----|
| 5 | Trade spotlight: Electrician | DITL electrician; "how to become an electrician in 5 steps"; apprentice salary graphic | `blog/how-to-become-an-electrician.html` |
| 6 | Trade spotlight: Welder + HVAC | Welder DITL; HVAC myth-bust; tool starter kit for welding | Trades pages + quiz |
| 7 | Trade school vs college math | "The real math" carousel; college debt vs apprenticeship earnings timeline | `blog/trade-school-vs-college.html` + `is-trade-school-worth-it.html` |
| 8 | Tools & apprenticeships | Tool review week (best beginner electrician tools); "how to find an apprenticeship"; apprentice-wages roundup | `blog/electrician-apprentice-salary.html` + resources.html |

### Phase 3 — Conversion (Weeks 9-12)

Goals: turn audience into quiz-takers and email subscribers. Success stories, "what's your trade" push, direct CTA language, story-link stickers daily.

| Week | Focus | Sample posts | CTA |
|------|-------|--------------|-----|
| 9 | Quiz promotion kickoff | "3 trades you didn't know existed — take the quiz"; story quiz + link sticker; 4 quiz-hook videos | **quiz.html (primary CTA)** |
| 10 | Success stories | "18, no college, welding" journey; testimonial/genericized story posts; blueprint your result card | Quiz + result share |
| 11 | Email capture push | "Free [Trade] Career Roadmap with your quiz result"; explainer of what the email contains; quiz countdown | **quiz.html email capture** |
| 12 | Social proof + consolidation | "Your trade = [X]" throwbacks; UGC repost; recap the 90 days; tease month 4 | Quiz + newsletter signup |

## 5. Engagement Strategy

### Comment templates (copy-paste)

**On trade-creator videos (DITL, apprenticeship Q&A):**
```
Apprentice electrician here ✋ what made you pick the trade over college?
```
```
Doing this for 2 years pays off faster than 4 years of debt. Facts only.
```
```
Wish someone showed me this at 16 — this is why we built TradeLift 🔥
```
**On salary/money discussions:**
```
Real numbers right here: electricians $60K-$80K, no degree required. 
```

### Collaboration ideas
- **Micro-influencers:** 1-5K follower trade apprentices (high bite, cheap/swap for promo). Offer: feature on TradeLift socials + quiz/shoutout in exchange for a DITL video + link.
- **Trade school & college councils:** co-create "trade school vs college" content (they bring credibility + audience).
- **Tool brands:** affiliate partnership content (starter kits) — aligns with affiliate plan in `docs/conversion-funnel.md`.
- **"Trade grams":** collab posts on day-in-the-life montages, each creator links to quiz.

### User-generated content (UGC) campaigns
- **#MyTradeQuiz:** users post their quiz result card from `social-sharing.md`; weekly feature + shoutout.
- **"Show me your setup":** weekly tool-bag/workshop photo roundups.
- **Apprenticeship Diaries:** recurring series, ask followers to submit wins (first paycheck, first callout) — reuse in Month-3 success stories.

## 6. Analytics & KPIs

### Follower targets (conservative, honest)

| Metric | End of Wk 4 | End of Wk 8 | End of Wk 12 |
|--------|-------------|-------------|--------------|
| TikTok followers | 500 | 1,500 | 3,000 |
| Instagram followers | 300 | 800 | 1,500 |
| X followers | 100 | 250 | 500 |
| YouTube Shorts subs | 50 | 150 | 300 |
| Total combined | ~950 | ~2,700 | ~5,300 |

### Engagement targets
- Engagement rate: ≥5% (likes+comments+shares / reach) on TikTok & IG
- Avg views per video: 300-800 in Phase 1 → 1,000-3,000 by Phase 3 (one "viral" breakout is a bonus, not the plan)
- Comments replied within 60 minutes of posting
- Link CTR: ≥2% on posts carrying a CTA

### Site traffic & email attribution
- **UTM scheme (base this site, not the retired github.io URLs):**
  ```
  https://tradelift.surge.sh/quiz.html?utm_source={platform}&utm_medium=social&utm_campaign={phase}&utm_content={post_type}
  ```
- Every in-bio link, story sticker, and QR uses a tracked UTM so GA4/Plausible shows per-platform performance.
- Weekly report: social sessions, quiz starts, quiz completions, email opt-ins.
- **Conversion funnel tracked:** social click → quiz start → completion → email opt-in. Report the drop-off each stage.

### Email signup attribution
- Primary signal: UTM-campaign filter ("quiz", "ph3_conversion", etc.) on opt-in traffic.
- Add a "How did you find us?" optional field to the quiz capture OR compare opt-in rates per UTM source.
- Target: 5-10% of quiz completers opt in (benchmark from `conversion-funnel.md`; aim above 12% by end of Phase 3).
- Newsletter signups driven by social-only CTAs get their own UTM (e.g., `utm_campaign=newsletter_x`).

### KPI review cadence
- **Daily (5 min):** reply to comments, note winning formats.
- **Weekly (30 min):** log followers, views, link clicks, opt-ins per platform in a tracker.
- **Day 30/60/90:** full report vs targets above; kill or double down per format.
