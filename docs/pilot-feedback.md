# TableText — Pilot Feedback Log

Record answers here after each week-2 and week-4 check-in with a pilot restaurant. One table per pilot.

## Week 2 check-in (5 questions)

1. **Signups/day** — how many new numbers in the last 2 weeks? (target: steady daily flow)
2. **Claim rate** — roughly how many guests actually showed their confirmation text to claim the free item? (%)
3. **Table engagement** — do servers/bus staff see guests scanning or asking about the QR tent? Any staff feedback?
4. **Blast response** — after the first weekly blast, any comments, redemptions, or pickup mentions tied to the text?
5. **Owner time** — how many minutes/week does the owner actually spend (approve drafts, reprint tents, etc.)?

**Act on answers:** low signups/day → move tents to check presenter + bar; low claim rate → shorten the claim step (photo of text, no code entry); dead blast → change offer timing to pre-weekend + Saturday reminder; high owner time → cut to 2 slots/wk and one-approval rule.
