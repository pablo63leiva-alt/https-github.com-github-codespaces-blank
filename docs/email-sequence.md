Honest answer: it depends on you. Apprenticeships pay as you learn but take 3–5 years to the
license. Trade school gets you trained faster and structured, but it **costs money** — figure out
total tuition, not the monthly payment they show you, before you sign.

We work with a few accredited trade schools so you can request program info **on one page, plainly

Before you click: read the placement rate and net tuition on their own site, then ask admissions
the same three questions: What does it really cost? What's the job-outcome rate? What do grads
actually earn?
