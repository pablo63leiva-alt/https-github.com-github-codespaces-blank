Explore the Trades

Finding the right trade for you starts with knowing your options. Browse in-demand careers, compare salaries, and discover the training path that fits your goals.

Trade Career Listings

⚡

Electrician

Construction
Average Salary: $60K–$80K
Time to Start: 4–5 year apprenticeship

Install, maintain, and repair electrical systems in homes, businesses, and industrial facilities. Electricians are essential to modern infrastructure and enjoy strong demand nationwide with opportunities for specialization and advancement.

Learn More
🔧

Plumber

Construction
Average Salary: $55K–$75K
Time to Start: 4–5 year apprenticeship

Install and repair water supply lines, drainage systems, and fixtures. Plumbers play a critical role in public health and construction, with year-round employment opportunities and the potential to run your own business.

Learn More
🔥

Welder

Industrial
Average Salary: $45K–$70K
Time to Start: 6 month–2 year certificate

Join metals together using various welding techniques for construction, manufacturing, and repair work. Welders work on everything from bridges and pipelines to aerospace components and custom fabrication projects.

Learn More
❄️

HVAC Technician

Construction
Average Salary: $50K–$70K
Time to Start: 2 year program

Install, maintain, and repair heating, ventilation, and air conditioning systems in residential and commercial buildings. HVAC technicians enjoy steady demand year-round due to climate control needs in every season.

Learn More
🚗

Automotive Mechanic

Automotive
Average Salary: $40K–$65K
Time to Start: 6 month–2 year program

Diagnose, repair, and maintain vehicles using both traditional mechanical skills and modern diagnostic technology. Automotive mechanics work in dealerships, independent shops, or start their own repair businesses.

Learn More
🪚

Carpenter

Construction
Average Salary: $45K–$65K
Time to Start: 3–4 year apprenticeship

Build, install, and repair structures and fixtures made of wood and other materials. Carpenters are the backbone of the construction industry, working on everything from framing houses to finishing custom cabinetry.

Learn More
🏗️

Ironworker

Industrial
Average Salary: $55K–$80K
Time to Start: 3 year apprenticeship

Erect structural and reinforcing iron and steel for buildings, bridges, and other large structures. Ironworkers are in high demand for major infrastructure projects and enjoy some of the best pay in the trades.

Learn More
🔩

Pipefitter

Industrial
Average Salary: $60K–$85K
Time to Start: 4–5 year apprenticeship

Assemble, install, and repair pipe systems in power plants, manufacturing facilities, and industrial settings. Pipefitters work with high-pressure systems and specialize in piping for steam, chemicals, and gases.

Learn More
🚛

Diesel Mechanic

Automotive
Average Salary: $45K–$65K
Time to Start: 2 year program

Inspect, repair, and maintain diesel engines in trucks, buses, and heavy equipment. Diesel mechanics are essential to keeping the transportation and construction industries running and often enjoy flexible work schedules.

Learn More
🧱

Mason

Skilled Craft
Average Salary: $45K–$70K
Time to Start: 3–4 year apprenticeship

Build structures and surfaces from brick, block, and stone. Masons are the skilled craftspeople behind walls, patios, foundations, and architectural details, with steady demand across residential and commercial construction.

Learn More
🏠

Roofer

Skilled Craft
Average Salary: $40K–$65K
Time to Start: 2–3 year apprenticeship

Install, repair, and replace roofs on residential and commercial buildings. Roofers practice a hands-on craft with year-round demand — every building needs a roof, and roofs wear out over time.

Learn More
📋

Construction Manager

Construction
Average Salary: $70K–$100K
Time to Start: Experience + promotions

Oversee construction projects from planning through completion, managing budgets, schedules, and crews. Many construction managers start as skilled tradespeople and work their way up with hands-on experience in the field.

Learn More
