Why Go Blue Collar?

The trades offer advantages that traditional 4-year degrees simply can't match.

💵

High Pay

Trade professionals earn competitive salaries that often rival or exceed white-collar positions. With experience and specialization, many tradespeople earn well over $80,000 annually. Overtime opportunities and union benefits can push earnings even higher.

🛡️

Job Security

With over 750,000 unfilled trade positions and a growing skills gap, skilled tradespeople are in constant demand. Infrastructure projects, housing development, and maintenance needs ensure stable employment regardless of economic conditions.

🎓

No Student Debt

Skip the massive student loans. Apprenticeships allow you to earn a wage while you learn, and many employers even cover your training costs. You'll graduate debt-free and immediately start building your career and savings.
