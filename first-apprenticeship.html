Straight Answers

Who is this playbook for?

It's written for 17–25 year olds getting their first construction or trades apprenticeship. If you're finishing high school, thinking about trade school, or already decided the trades are for you but don't know how to actually get hired, this walks you through it step by step.

Will this get me an apprenticeship?

Nothing can guarantee you a job in any field. What this guide does is remove the guesswork: the exact 5-step application order, copy-paste resume and cover letter templates for union and non-union, what to study for the aptitude test, and 8 interview questions with answers to rehearse. It's the preparation that most applicants never do.

Does it cover union and non-union apprenticeships?

Yes. The playbook includes a full union vs non-union comparison (pay structure, training quality, $0-cost vs fees, and mobility) plus separate cover letter templates for union halls and non-union shops, so you can apply to both routes.

How is the pay data sourced?

Every figure comes from TradeLift's published pages, which draw on BLS and industry data. Apprentice wages by year match the site's apprentice-wages-by-year post, and journey-level bands match the per-trade salary posts for each spot shown. Nothing in the guide invents a statistic.
