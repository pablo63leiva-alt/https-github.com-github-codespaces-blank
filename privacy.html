Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters to us. This page explains what data TradeLift collects, how it's used, and the choices you have.

Effective date: September 15, 2026. Last updated: September 15, 2026.

1. Information We Collect

We collect only what you voluntarily provide: your email address when you sign up for our newsletter, submit the career quiz email form, or use the exit-intent popup to download the free Career Fit Guide. If you contact us directly, we'll have whatever you include in that message. We do not collect payment info or any other sensitive personal data.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your email is used to send the guide or roadmap you requested, deliver occasional career tips, and respond to your questions. We will never sell, rent, or trade your email to third parties for marketing.

3. Cookies & Local Storage

TradeLift uses browser local storage (not cookies) for basic functionality:

tradelift_exit_modal_dismissed — hides the exit popup after you close it.
tradelift_exit_modal_converted — records that you signed up via the popup.
tradelift_exit_modal_session_shown — limits the popup to once per session.
tradelift_capture_events — logs quiz email-capture events for internal analytics.

No advertising cookies are set today. If we later add Google Analytics or AdSense, cookies may be placed by those services and we will update this page and add a consent mechanism where required.

4. Analytics & Advertising (AdSense)

If Google Analytics or AdSense is enabled, third-party vendors may use cookies to collect your IP address, browser type, device info, and browsing activity to serve ads and measure performance. You can opt out via Google Ads Settings or www.aboutads.info. See Google's partner-sites policy for details on how Google uses data.

5. Email List & Third-Party Services

Email submissions are processed by Formspree so we can deliver the content you asked for. We may move to a dedicated provider like Buttondown in the future; if so, data will be migrated and this policy updated. We do not sell your data.

6. Affiliate Disclosure

TradeLift participates in the Amazon Services LLC Associates Program. Some links are affiliate links — we may earn a small commission at no extra cost to you. This does not influence our recommendations.

7. Data Protection for Teens / COPPA

TradeLift is designed for students and young adults aged 16+. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If we learn we have, we'll delete it immediately. Parents or guardians who believe their child submitted data may contact us below.

8. Your Rights (CCPA / GDPR)

You have the right to access, correct, delete, or opt out of processing of your personal data, regardless of where you live. To exercise any right, email hello@tradelift.surge.sh. We respond within 30 days.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this page from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top will always reflect the most recent revision. Continued use of the site after changes are posted means you accept the updated policy.

10. Contact

Questions? Reach out at hello@tradelift.surge.sh. Published by TradeLift.
