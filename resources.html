Resources for Future Tradespeople

Everything you need to jumpstart your trade career — from essential tools and must-have certifications to the best YouTube channels, books, and websites for learning the trades.

Essential Starter Tools

You don't need a $5,000 toolbox on day one. Start with these fundamentals and build your collection over time.

🔨

Hand Tools

16 oz hammer (Estwing), 25' tape measure (Stanley FatMax), channel-lock pliers, flathead & Phillips screwdriver set, utility knife, torpedo level. Budget: $80-$150.

🦺

Safety Gear

Hard hat (Class E), ANSI Z87.1 safety glasses, steel-toe work boots, leather work gloves. Never skimp on PPE — your safety is non-negotiable. Budget: $100-$200.

🧰

Tool Storage

16"+ tool bag with multiple pockets or a leather tool belt. Keeps your essentials organized and within reach. Look for reinforced stitching. Budget: $30-$80.

🧾

Starter Kits for 12 Trades

Trade-by-trade starter tool lists with realistic budgets for electricians, plumbers, welders, HVAC techs, and more. See exactly what to buy before day one.

Must-Have Certifications

These credentials prove you're serious and qualified. Get them early to stand out from the competition.

🛡️

OSHA 10-Hour

Safety

The #1 entry-level certification. Covers hazard recognition, fall protection, electrical safety, and emergency response. Required on nearly every construction site. Complete in 2 days online or in-person for $25-$75.

🏥

First Aid / CPR

Safety

Job sites are dangerous — knowing how to respond to an emergency could save a life. Offered by the Red Cross and American Heart Association. Complete in a single day for $50-$100.

✅

OSHA 30-Hour

Advanced Safety

The advanced safety credential for supervisors and foremen. Shows employers you understand safety management and regulatory compliance. Essential for career advancement into leadership roles.

⚡

Trade-Specific Certs

Trade-Specific

EPA 608 (HVAC), AWS (welding), NEC knowledge (electrical), state plumbing licenses. Research which certs are most valued in your local market and prioritize accordingly.

Best YouTube Channels for Trades

Learn from experienced tradespeople for free. These channels offer real-world knowledge you won't find in textbooks.

🎬

Electrician U

Dustin Stelzer breaks down electrical concepts in an easy-to-understand way. Perfect for aspiring electricians who want to learn the theory and practical skills behind the trade.

🎬

Got2Learn

Comprehensive plumbing tutorials from basic repairs to advanced installations. Great visual explanations that make complex plumbing systems easy to understand.

🎬

Weld.com

Professional welding instruction covering MIG, TIG, stick, and flux-core processes. From beginner techniques to advanced certifications, this channel covers it all.

🎬

HVAC School

In-depth HVAC training from Bryant gases to refrigerant recovery. Bryan Orr makes complex HVAC topics accessible and practical for technicians at every level.

🎬

Essential Craftsman

High-quality construction content covering everything from framing to finish work. Scott Wadsworth's attention to detail and production quality make learning construction engaging.

🎬

See Jane Drill

Beginner-friendly home improvement and construction tutorials. Perfect for people just starting out who want to build foundational skills with clear, patient instruction.

Recommended Books

Deepen your knowledge with these essential reads for anyone serious about a career in the trades.

📖

Code Check Series

The essential reference for electrical, plumbing, and building codes. These pocket-sized guides help you understand and comply with local building regulations on every job.

📖

Audel Electrician's Library

Comprehensive electrical reference covering wiring, motors, controls, and more. A must-have for electricians and anyone working near electrical systems.

📖

Ugly's Electrical References

The pocket reference every electrician carries on the job. Quick-lookup charts for calculations, wiring diagrams, and code requirements. Compact and indispensable.

📖

Plumbing 101

Foundational plumbing knowledge covering residential and commercial systems. Great for apprentices or anyone wanting to understand the fundamentals of water supply, drainage, and fixtures.

Useful Websites & Tools

Online resources to help you find apprenticeships, study for exams, and connect with the trade community.

🌐

Apprenticeship.gov

The official U.S. government site for finding registered apprenticeship programs. Search by trade, state, and zip code. The best starting point for finding structured earn-while-you-learn programs.

🌐

OSHA Training Portal

Find OSHA-authorized training providers for your 10-hour and 30-hour certifications. Many providers offer online courses at affordable rates. Verify providers at osha.gov.

🌐

UnionPlus.org

Resources for union members and aspiring union tradespeople. Find local union halls, learn about benefits, and access training opportunities through the AFL-CIO network.

🌐

Build Your Future (NAHB)

The National Association of Home Builders' career resource. Explore construction trades, find training programs, and learn about career paths in residential construction.
